Brand book · v0.2

Field notes

How The Next Research App looks, and why. A notebook kept by a research team: warm paper, dark ink, content on cards, and a vermilion accent for what to notice.

Principles

  • Academic, not commercial. We describe; we don't sell. Plain sentences, no superlatives, no stock photos, no gradients.
  • Made by hand, visibly. Hairline rules, numbered sections, captions in mono: the conventions of a paper, used with care.
  • One signal per view. Vermilion marks what to notice: a section number, a list marker, a label. Never a block of body text.
  • Change the tokens, not the pages. Every colour, face, size and radius is a token in section 1 of main.css. This page redraws itself from them.

Colour

Three families. Neutrals carry the page. Forest is the institution: buttons, links, the footer. Signal is the accent, used sparingly.

  • The page is --color-bg (paper); cards and inputs sit on --color-surface, a lighter paper.
  • Text is ink; secondary text is graphite. Signal is never body text: only numbers, markers and labels.
  • Forest-deep is the one dark ground: the footer and the "Have a study in mind?" banner.
  • Tints (sage, signal-tint) are for panels and selection, never for text.

Type

A serif to state things, a sans to read, a mono to annotate. Sizes come from one base and one ratio (--type-base, --type-ratio), so changing either rescales the whole site.

Motif

Parked: the signal trace, a flat line, two bursts of movement, then a wandering location track. It is switched off for now; set --trace-display to block to bring it back under the hero, along the study banner, across the footer edge and as the logo mark.

In use: numbered section headings (01), lettered lists, figures on graph paper captioned "Fig.", and a faint paper grain (--grain-opacity, 0 to switch it off).

  • One primary button per view. It carries the signal offset; pressing it closes the gap.
  • Content sits on cards: lighter paper, a hairline border, a soft shadow and a 6px radius. Every card on the site takes these from the card tokens (--card-bg, --card-border, --card-radius, --card-padding, --card-shadow).
  • Images sit at 4px radius. Until an image exists, its description shows as a captioned figure.

Voice

  • Write for researchers: plain, second person, short declarative sentences.
  • Say what the app does and how it was studied. Avoid "powerful", "seamless", "unlock", "revolutionary".
  • Numbers carry their base and status: "X of Y invited participants", "preliminary".
